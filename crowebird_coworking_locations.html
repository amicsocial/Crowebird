Coworking location candidates for Crowebird Plumbing

Most of the high-income homeowners in Crowebird’s part of Southwest Florida live in three clusters: southeast Cape Coral, Bonita Springs at the Collier County line, and Estero. One office in each reaches about 36,400 households earning $150,000 or more. The Cape Coral circle shares none of them with the other two.

Bonita SpringsImperial Pkwy at Bonita Beach Rd

Keep in mind: more than half of those households are just across the county line in North Naples, and the best office there (Regus on South Bay Drive, card 4) still needs month-to-month confirmed.

How the towns compare

Every location is measured with the same 5-mile circle. “Only in this circle” counts the high-income households that the other two picks do not also reach.

How the three were chosen

Every point on a quarter-mile grid across Lee County was tested as a circle center, looking for the three circles that together cover the most households earning $150,000 or more. Households in Collier and Charlotte counties count when a circle reaches them, but every center had to be in Lee County. The best possible set reaches about 38,600. Moving each center to a named spot nearby keeps about 36,400, within 6% of that.

Cape Coral

The circle on Del Prado Blvd S covers South Cape, the Cape Coral Pkwy and Del Prado corridors, Cape Harbour and, across the river, the McGregor Blvd side of South Fort Myers. It holds about 13,900 households earning $150,000 or more, the most of the three, and 28% of its homes were built before 1980, roughly three times the share in the other two circles. It is also the busiest: 27 plumbers list a street address inside it, mostly small Cape Coral shops, so each has about 510 high-income households to draw from. It shares no high-income households with the other two picks.

Bonita Springs

Centered where Imperial Pkwy meets Bonita Beach Rd beside I-75, this circle takes in Bonita Springs, Pelican Landing and the north end of Naples. About 7,700 of its 13,600 high-income households live on the Collier County side of the line. Only 7 plumbers list a street address within 5 miles, none of them a national franchise, so it is the easiest of the three to rank in. Its homes are newer (8% built before 1980) and 79% are owner-occupied.

Estero

The Estero circle, centered on US-41 at Corkscrew Rd by Koreshan State Park, covers Estero, San Carlos Park, Three Oaks and the US-41 corridor north toward Alico Rd. It holds about 10,700 high-income households, and 82% of its households own their home, the highest share of the three. About 1,700 of its high-income households are also in the Bonita Springs circle. 9 plumbers list a street address inside it, including a Zoom Drain franchise, and none is closer than 3.5 miles to the center: they cluster in the Alico Rd business parks at the north edge of the circle.

Pros and cons at a glance

Cape Coral

Pros

  • Largest market: 13,900 high-income households and 81,100 households in total.
  • Oldest housing: 28% built before 1980.
  • No overlap with the other two circles.

Cons

  • Most competition: 27 plumbers, about 510 high-income households each.
  • Lowest share of high-income households (17%).
  • Part of the circle is the Caloosahatchee River.

Bonita Springs

Pros

  • Best ratio: 7 plumbers, about 1,940 high-income households each.
  • 30% of households earn $150k+.
  • No national franchise with a street address in the circle.

Cons

  • About 7,700 of its high-income households are in Collier County.
  • Newer homes (8% built before 1980).
  • Shares about 1,700 high-income households with Estero.

Estero

Pros

  • Second-best ratio: 9 plumbers, about 1,180 high-income households each.
  • Highest homeownership of the three (82%).
  • Entirely in Lee County.

Cons

  • Smallest market: 10,700 high-income households, 36,500 in total.
  • Newer homes (10% built before 1980).
  • A Zoom Drain franchise sits inside the circle.

Other towns that were considered

These are good markets on their own, but once the three picks are in place most of their high-income households are already covered, or the market is small.

Cape Coral

Cape Coral has the best set of the three areas. Coral Breeze comes first and is the best coworking option in the report: its own site states month-to-month pricing with no lease, weekday receptionist hours, and the suite is in a one-storey plaza. Bonita Springs

All three have staffed reception, but none has month-to-month confirmed. Regus comes first because its site says there is no minimum term, although another Regus page contradicts that. Windsor is the only confirmed one-storey entry, but its office listings are a year or more old. Riverview has one office listed as available; ask which floor it is on.

Estero

Only two offices within 5 miles of the Estero center pass the entry walk, so the third slot goes to the best near-fail: Coconut Point Executive Center is close and well priced, but it is upstairs (red). The Brooks has a receptionist but shows no vacancy today. Creative Executive Space is the only office in any area that confirms the tenant's name goes on the entrance door. Regus and Riverview, listed under Bonita Springs, are also within about 6 miles of Estero.

What to ask on each call

  1. Can the company name go on a lobby or building directory from the first month? Almost no listing publishes this.
  2. Can the company name go on the office door itself, not just a monument sign? This is also almost never published.
  3. Is the office available now, and will you put month-to-month with no minimum in writing?
  4. Is the office on the ground floor, with its own door from the parking lot or a short walk from the front entrance?
  5. Is the front desk staffed during business hours, will mail addressed to the suite be accepted, and can someone confirm the address by video or in person?

How this was put together

Business confirmed. Crowebird Plumbing & Drains is an active Google Business Profile client in monday.com (GMB/Site package), with Michael Crowebird as the contact at (239) 286-8946. Its Thumbtack profile places it in Lehigh Acres and describes its service area as Southwest Florida. No company website was found to cross-check the phone number. No area was treated as already covered.

Circle centers. Cape Coral: 3700 Del Prado Blvd S. Bonita Springs: 28100 Imperial Pkwy, at Bonita Beach Rd beside I-75. Estero: Koreshan State Park, 3800 Corkscrew Rd at US-41. All three were placed with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder.

Choosing the towns. Every point on a quarter-mile grid in Lee County was scored on the number of $150,000+ households within 5 miles, and the three centers that together cover the most of them (each household counted once) were found by search. The best set reaches about 38,600; the three named centers reach about 36,400. Centers were limited to Lee County, the area the earlier office list covered; households in Collier and Charlotte counties still count when a circle reaches them.

Households. U.S. Census Bureau American Community Survey 2020 to 2024 5-year estimates by census tract for Lee, Collier and Charlotte counties: household income (table B19001, the $150,000 to $199,999 and $200,000+ brackets), owner-occupied homes (B25003) and year the structure was built (B25034). Each tract’s households were spread evenly over its land, with bays, rivers and lakes from Census TIGERweb removed, and then counted inside each 5-mile circle.

Competitors. Better Business Bureau listings in the Plumber category, searched by distance from Fort Myers, Cape Coral, Estero, Bonita Springs, Naples, Lehigh Acres and North Fort Myers (456 listings). A business counts if its name or main category is plumbing, drain, sewer, rooter or water heater and it lists a street address; 41 listings outside the trade, 221 with no street address, 28 PO boxes and 4 private mailboxes were dropped. Addresses were placed with the Census geocoder, because many BBB map points sit on ZIP code centers. Distances are exact; the lists round them to a tenth of a mile.

Offices. Small private offices within about 6 miles of each center, found on LoopNet, Cityfeet, Crexi, OfficeSpace.com, CoworkingCafe, CommercialCafe, CommercialSearch, Office Hub, Instant Offices, LiquidSpace, MLS listings and operator pages, checked November 16, 2026. Offices from the October 13 list were re-checked; where a term confirmed then no longer appears online, the card says so. Craigslist could not be checked this time. Addresses were placed with the Census geocoder, and distances are straight-line from each circle center.

Map. Census tracts from the 2022 cartographic boundary files (via CitySDK); freeways, highways, major roads and water from Census TIGERweb. Shading is households earning $150,000 or more per square mile of land. The color scale runs from 5 to 25 up to 320 and more per square mile, higher than the default scale, because Lee County’s densities are higher.
